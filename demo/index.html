    Your data

    Your workouts are stored in this browser on this device. There is no central workout database or automatic cloud upload. You choose when to export a copy and where to save it.

    How saving works

    Weight starts from your latest recorded session; it is a starting point, not an automatic weight increase. Save every set during your rest. “Saved” means the device confirmed it. If saving fails, retry before leaving this screen.

    “Skip” leaves this set unlogged. The checklist and finish check will remind you. One workout per calendar day is supported. This page needs an internet connection; it cannot start offline or send background reminders while your phone is locked.
