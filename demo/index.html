    How saving works

    Weight starts from your latest recorded session; it is a starting point, not an automatic weight increase. Save every set during your rest. “Saved” means the spreadsheet confirmed it. If saving fails, retry before leaving this screen.

    You can also use your existing Google Form. Keep its Round field. Form entries appear after refreshing. Logger entries take precedence for the same day, exercise and round.

    “Skip” leaves this set unlogged. The checklist and finish check will remind you. One workout per calendar day is supported. This page needs an internet connection; it cannot start offline or send background reminders while your phone is locked.
